About

Hi there! It’s me, Alex! I’m a data analyst with a background in data visualization and a focus on public interest work.

I earned my MS in Data Analysis and Visualization from CUNY Graduate Center and previously studied Studio Art and Mathematics at Skidmore College.

Before transitioning into data, I spent several years working as an artist assistant and art handler across Brooklyn. That experience shaped how I approach data today—bringing creativity, attention to detail, and a love for turning complex ideas into something clear and meaningful.

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with loved ones, cuddling with my cat, and the Law & Order theme song.
